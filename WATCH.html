Ottawa Fleet in two minutes

A synthetic Ottawa fleet, durable ride assignment, and server-controlled trips. These short Playwright recordings have on-screen captions and no audio.

Recorded examples, not live status. Read the quick guide or check the captured release and outcomes.

1. Read the fleet

See the neighborhood counts and a deliberately stale vehicle. Unknown means the observation is too old to treat as current.

2. Follow a trip, then refresh

One bounded trip continues on the server. Refresh restores its run identity. A vehicle assignment and a completed passenger trip are different milestones.

3. Stop and drain

Stop new work while the already accepted trip finishes. Look for zero incomplete trips, requests and events at the end.
